Privacy Policy

Pin Stats Tracker

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Purpose

Pin Stats Tracker is a personal project intended to help its owner monitor and organize analytics from their own Pinterest business account.

Information Accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access Pinterest account information, Pin information, and analytics made available through Pinterest's API. This may include impressions, engagements, saves, Pin clicks, outbound clicks, and other available metrics.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to organize and review Pinterest performance in the owner's private Google Sheets spreadsheet.

Storage and Sharing

The project is designed for personal use. It does not sell or rent personal information or Pinterest analytics to third parties. Data storage and access depend on the connected services and the application's implementation.

Data Security

Reasonable steps will be taken to protect account access credentials and collected information. Users should keep their account credentials and authorization tokens secure.

Data Deletion and Revoking Access

The account owner can delete information stored in their own spreadsheet and revoke the application's access through their Pinterest account settings.
